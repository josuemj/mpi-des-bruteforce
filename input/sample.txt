It was a quiet Tuesday morning, a day that felt less like a beginning and more like a gentle continuation of the past week's rhythm. The scent of freshly brewed coffee mingled with the faint, sweet smell of a blooming jasmine plant on the windowsill, an unexpected and welcome fragrance in the urban air.
